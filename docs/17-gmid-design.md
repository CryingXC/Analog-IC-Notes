# 17 · \(g_m/I_D\) Design

The ratio

\[
\frac{g_m}{I_D}
\]

is a useful measure of transconductance efficiency.

Qualitative regions:

- **weak inversion:** high \(g_m/I_D\), low speed per unit capacitance;
- **moderate inversion:** balanced efficiency / speed;
- **strong inversion:** lower \(g_m/I_D\), often higher current density and speed.

Instead of assuming square-law equations are accurate, modern gm/Id workflows use transistor characterization data to map:
- \(g_m/I_D\);
- current density;
- \(g_m/g_{ds}\);
- capacitances;
- \(f_T\);
- inversion level.

The method is process-specific in data, but process-independent in reasoning.
