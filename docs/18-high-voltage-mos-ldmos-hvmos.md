# 18 · HVMOS / LDMOS

High-voltage MOS devices are engineered to reduce peak electric field.

## LDMOS intuition

A lateral drift region between channel and drain spreads voltage over a longer physical distance. The effective high-field region is therefore not identical to the short channel of a core MOS device.

Trade-offs:
- higher breakdown voltage;
- larger area;
- higher on-resistance;
- larger parasitic capacitance.

## HVMOS wells

Deep / extended well structures reshape depletion regions and isolate high-field drain regions from sensitive junctions.

This is why high-voltage device cross-sections often look asymmetric: the drain side may receive a long drift / well extension while the source side stays closer to a conventional body connection.

## Important distinction

"Effective channel length" in a high-voltage device may refer to different physical intervals depending on the compact model, layout convention, or field-shaping structure. Always read the authorized device documentation rather than inferring it only from a cartoon cross-section.
