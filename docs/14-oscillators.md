# 14 · Oscillators

The Barkhausen intuition at the oscillation frequency is

\[
|A\beta|\ge1,\qquad \angle A\beta \approx 0^\circ \pmod{360^\circ}
\]

## Ring oscillator

For \(N\) inverting delay stages:

\[
f_{osc}\approx \frac{1}{2Nt_d}
\]

This makes delay, supply voltage, process and temperature directly visible in frequency.

## Startup

Noise or initial asymmetry grows if small-signal loop gain is sufficient.

## Key metrics

- frequency;
- tuning range;
- phase noise / jitter;
- startup time;
- power;
- supply sensitivity;
- PVT spread.
